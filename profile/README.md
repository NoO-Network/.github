# Niche of One

I build on land I own.

Everyone else is out here trying to build the next TikTok. I'm building the strip mall of dollar stores: useful, entertaining, or educational things for the waitress, the construction worker, the retail kid and his mom at the factory. My people. The ones the premium-course crowd priced out on purpose.

One person, no team. Field manuals, weird fiction, and the tools I got tired of waiting for a company to build.

No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no firehose of ten products wearing ten different hats. What's listed is what it is, built by the guy selling it.

If you make good things and want a door with your name on it, reach out. I'm building the storefront either way.
